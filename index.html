Characters

Matthew “Matt” McKay

Head of McKay Industries. “He looked like a pinkish walrush. Cherubic.” Ch. 1 Husband of Martha Anne; father of the late Peter and of Linus, Clementine, Cletus, and Evarista; brother of Alexia.

  • Ch. 1 Meets Angelic and receives her warning.
  • Ch. 2 Hires Curlee after Red Dog wins.
  • Ch. 5 Organizes the vigil over Linus.
  • Ch. 15 Learns of Alexia’s secret marriage.
  • Ch. 16 Hears Kirol’s account of Alexia’s crimes.

Martha Anne McKay

Wife of Matthew; mother of Peter, Linus, Clementine, Cletus, and Evarista. Peter died at ten. She calls Evarista “the Dragon’s Child” Ch. 4.

Alexia McKay

Matthew’s sister and owner of the family house. She built much of the family fortune; Garrity says that she and Matt now hold equal shares in the company Chs. 2, 12, 16. The narrator describes her as “a renaissance woman,” with “a wide range to her” and “startling discords in the middle register” Ch. 4. She keeps a laboratory in her room; Matt says she “knows all that is to be known about the history of venoms” Ch. 5.

Family

  • Secretly married Basil Saint-Lambert, later known as Kirol Lazzari, more than twenty years before the present action Chs. 9, 13, 16.
  • Mother of Angelic, born in Greece Chs. 9, 16.
  • Was confined in an asylum in Messina after attacking her infant daughter; escaped months later Ch. 16.

Actions

  • Ch. 3 Says her premonition does not concern Linus; compares Angelic to Joshua bringing down Curlee’s defenses.
  • Ch. 6 Deals ominous cards for almost everyone except Linus.
  • Ch. 9 Describes her honeymoon attack on Kirol: she beat his head, cut him, and left him for dead. She presents him as a persecutor who possessed her.
  • Ch. 11 Accuses Kirol of attacking Evarista.
  • Ch. 14 Christy recalls her former nickname, La Sorcière.
  • Ch. 15 Attacks Clementine in the upstairs parlor; Kirol intervenes.

Kirol’s account in Chapter 16

  • She poisoned Cletus by mixing lethal pellets with his mild sleeping pills.
  • She inflicted Evarista’s twenty-seven knife wounds.
  • She repeatedly tried to kill Angelic: twice in infancy, again at three or four, again at about ten, and once more during the present visit.
  • She arranged Garrity’s attack on Kirol close to nineteen years earlier.

Peter McKay

Matt and Martha Anne’s older son, who died at ten. He is remembered in a small pencil sketch Ch. 4.

Linus McKay

Eldest surviving son. Coralee reports Ginny’s nickname for him, the “Lost Dauphin,” because he is heir to the family business and “he sure is lost” Ch. 2. He considers suicide “very unlikely” Ch. 3. The family’s vigil centers on him Chs. 5–6.

Clementine McKay

Eldest daughter. Wonders why Cletus never brings his girlfriends home Ch. 6. Alexia attacks her in the upstairs parlor; Kirol frees her from Alexia’s grip before Curlee opens the door Ch. 15.

Cletus McKay

Youngest son, seventeen. The “brightest and most prospective son” in Angelic’s warning Chs. 1, 16. Described as “a poetry-scribbling quarterback, a cataclysmic practical joker, a gloomy philosopher and hilarious courter” Ch. 4. His romances have lasted at most twenty-seven days Ch. 6. He is seeing Angelic. He dies late Wednesday night despite Doctor Holland’s efforts Ch. 6. The pellets found in his hand include a lethal poison Ch. 7.

Evarista McKay

Youngest daughter, fourteen Ch. 2. Martha Anne calls her the “Dragon’s Child” Ch. 4. She resembles Angelic, who is her first cousin Ch. 13.

  • Ch. 5 Recites Cletus’s quatrain ending “And slay the monster in his lair.”
  • Chs. 10–11 Is discovered grievously wounded.
  • Ch. 12 Gives the wound count: twenty-seven.
  • Ch. 13 Describes Kirol healing her: “He touched me with his hands, with his hands, with his hands. And he healed me that way. He really did.”
  • Ch. 13 Discusses Fabre and the mantis’s hold over its victims. Eventually identifies her attacker to Curlee.

Kirol Lazzari / Basil Saint-Lambert

Fortune-teller, mentalist, and healer. Married Alexia under the name Basil Saint-Lambert Ch. 9. Third cousin of Matthew and Alexia, and Angelic’s father Ch. 16.

  • Ch. 8 Appears in Alexia’s recollections as Basil, a busboy, theater rigger, and poet.
  • Ch. 11 Is accused by Alexia of attacking Evarista.
  • Ch. 13 Is revealed to have healed Evarista.
  • Ch. 15 Rescues Clementine and holds Alexia’s wrists until Alice takes charge of her.
  • Ch. 16 Recounts Alexia’s history and explains how he prompted Angelic’s warning. Calls Christy a “toy vulture.” Leaves his own means of entering the house unresolved.

Angelic Theresa Lazzari

Fortune-teller; daughter of Kirol and Alexia. Born in Greece twenty-two years earlier Chs. 6, 9. She describes herself as Irish and Romany and says she also plays Latin identities in her work Ch. 1.

  • Ch. 1 Reads Garrity and delivers the warning to Matt.
  • Ch. 3 Dines with Curlee and reports her mother’s threat to kill her.
  • Ch. 7 Describes the two childhood attacks she remembers. She and Kirol were held for eighteen hours after Cletus’s death and released for his funeral. She later slips away from Curlee.
  • Ch. 10 Is found at the Lazzaris’ camp and taken to the police.
  • Ch. 13 Learns from Curlee that Alexia is her mother and identifies her attacker to him.
  • Ch. 16 Kirol explains her kinship with Cletus and her willingness to care for Alexia.

Carl Curlee

Independent investigator with an office in the McKay Building beside the firm’s Legal Department. “Slight, sandy, convivial, celibate” and ranked among the world’s ten best puzzle-solvers Ch. 2.

  • Ch. 2 Is retained after Red Dog wins.
  • Ch. 3 Tests Angelic with the name “Great Mogo,” which he claims to have invented. She insists Mogo was real and that Curlee took the name from her mind.
  • Ch. 4 Studies his seven portraits of the family and suspects that the danger comes from within it.
  • Ch. 12 Draws Kirol as he would have looked in his youth.
  • Chs. 13–15 Obtains the victims’ testimony and arranges the final confrontation.
  • Ch. 16 Draws a “goat song” while Kirol tells his story.

Virginia “Ginny” Bishop

Matt’s secretary. Described as “large and good-looking” Ch. 2. Calls Linus the “Lost Dauphin” and tells Curlee there has always been something wrong with the McKays.

Coralee Landers

Curlee’s office assistant. Gathers background on the McKays Ch. 2. Declares, “I am the best gossip in town, not the worst” Ch. 12. Mistakes Curlee’s sketch of young Kirol for Cletus Ch. 13.

Gatian Garrity

McKay Industries employee. Angelic addresses him as “Garrison” Ch. 1; Garrity is not his original name Ch. 15.

  • Ch. 12 Admits moving money for Alexia to pay a blackmailer.
  • Ch. 15 Confesses that he attempted to kill Kirol on Alexia’s orders nineteen years earlier and served time in prison.

Doctor Holland

The McKay family physician. Has resuscitated more than fifty people after suicide attempts Ch. 5. Monitors Linus during the vigil, then works unsuccessfully on Cletus for fifteen minutes Ch. 6.

Johnny Correo

Reporter for the Morning Telegraph. Supplies background on the Lazzaris and prepares a report of the expected death Ch. 6.

Thomas Christy

A pharmacist with medical training and an old acquaintance of Alexia. His father and the McKays’ father were friends in different branches of the drug business Chs. 8, 13. Kirol calls him a “toy vulture” and a “junior vulture” Ch. 16.

  • Ch. 13 Introduces himself to Curlee and confirms Alexia’s secret marriage.
  • Ch. 14 Recognizes Kirol’s early handwriting in a poem attributed to Cletus.
  • Ch. 15 Is exposed as Alexia’s blackmailer.

Lolly Lancaster

A friend of Curlee’s at Keeleys. Supplies the Romany words yag, sarishan, dukkerin, and Rani. Takes him to her uncle, Red-Eye Reilly, who calls Little Willy Ringo Ch. 10.

Little Willy Ringo

An acquaintance of Curlee’s who takes him to the Lazzaris’ camp. Pretends to follow patteran signs but admits he already knew where the Lazzaris were and cannot read the signs Ch. 10.

Crotchett

A police laboratory expert. Estimates that the handwritten poem attributed to Cletus is about twenty years old Ch. 12.

Alice

The police matron assigned to the McKay house after Evarista’s stabbing Ch. 11. Alexia knocks her down before attacking Clementine. Alice recovers and takes charge of Alexia Ch. 15.

Chapter Synopses

Chapter 1A red-haired fortune-telling witch

Virginia announces Angelic’s arrival. Angelic addresses Garrity as “Garrison” and describes his embezzlements and troubled conscience. She places Matt’s ten-dollar bet on Red Dog at seven to one and pays his winnings in advance. She warns him that his brightest and most prospective son will apparently commit suicide late the next night. The son is unnamed; Matt assumes she means Linus.

Chapter 2A horseless knight, a mannered squire,

Red Dog wins. Matt retains Curlee. Virginia tells him that something has always been wrong with the McKays. Coralee gathers background and reports Ginny’s nickname for Linus, the “Lost Dauphin.” Curlee drives to the family house.

Chapter 3A threat, a riddle, and a sign,

At dinner, Curlee asks Linus whether he plans to kill himself; Linus finds it unlikely. Alexia’s premonition concerns someone else. She compares Angelic to Joshua, predicting that she will break down Curlee’s defenses. Curlee takes Angelic to Keeleys. He tests her with the name “Great Mogo,” which he says he invented; she insists the magician existed. She reports her mother’s threat to kill her.

Chapter 4The faces glow, the minutes crawl,

Curlee considers seven portraits of the McKays and the pencil sketch of Peter, who died at ten. He suspects that the family’s danger comes from within. Martha Anne’s question about Evarista leads to the nickname “Dragon’s Child.” Matt fears for Linus, and Curlee spends Wednesday guarding him at the office.

Chapter 5The odds are chalked, the hour grows late,

Matt locks the house and initially refuses to let Cletus and Clementine go out, but yields to Alexia’s insistence. Evarista recites Cletus’s poetry, including the promise to slay a monster. The family gathers in a stripped parlor to watch Linus. Doctor Holland arrives and discusses suicide and resuscitation. Matt describes the family’s chemical business and Alexia’s knowledge of venoms.

Chapter 6With rheumy eyes and stoppled breath,

Correo arrives with information about the Lazzaris. A storm approaches. Alexia deals ominous cards for almost everyone except Linus. Holland checks the sleeping Linus. Cletus returns drenched and dazed after seeing Angelic, then goes upstairs. Curlee breaks into Cletus’s locked room and finds him drowsy on the bed. Holland works on him for fifteen minutes, but Cletus dies.

Chapter 7To follow close, behind, before;

Cletus is buried. The pellets found in his hand include two nearly harmless compounds and a third that would be fatal almost instantly. Curlee talks with Angelic after her eighteen-hour detention with her father. She describes two remembered attacks by a “monster,” one surviving as a childhood nightmare and the other occurring when she was about ten. She recognizes the attacker but withholds the name, then slips away from Curlee.

Chapter 8The change that comes, the person hid

Alexia accuses Kirol of blackmail. Pressed by Curlee, she begins the history of her New York circle, including Basil Saint-Lambert and Thomas Christy. She describes Basil’s varied occupations, their attraction, and the courtship that led to marriage.

Chapter 9A mad-man ghost, a voice that cries

Alexia says she married Basil, who adopted the name Kirol Lazzari. She describes the honeymoon as a period of possession and torment. She recounts beating and cutting him, then fleeing before dawn on the fifth day, convinced he was dead. After nine months of wandering, she gives birth to Angelic in Greece. Her account portrays Kirol as the child’s would-be killer. She dates their final separation, after a further clouded period, to about twenty-one years earlier.

Chapter 10Should horror wrap you like a cloak

Evarista produces another poem attributed to Cletus and fears she will be the next victim. Lolly Lancaster explains several Romany words and takes Curlee to her uncle, who calls Little Willy Ringo. Willy leads Curlee to the Lazzaris’ camp, pretending to follow patteran signs although he already knows the location. Curlee takes Angelic to the police and looks up mantis in the dictionary. At the McKay house that night, Alexia announces that Evarista is dying.

Chapter 11Of stab and slash. Of mind that raved.

Evarista has been stabbed more than twenty times. Alexia says she saw Kirol attack her. Curlee discovers pipe ash and four spent matches on the roof. Evarista is taken to the hospital, and the police assign the matron Alice to the house.

Chapter 12The Witch is held in durance vile.

Evarista reports twenty-seven wounds and many bruises but refuses to name her attacker. Garrity admits transferring money for Alexia to pay a blackmailer. Crotchett estimates that the handwritten poem is about twenty years old. Curlee draws Kirol as a young man and visits Angelic in jail.

Chapter 13Of healing hands and Gordian knot,

Evarista describes Kirol’s healing touch. She and Curlee discuss the mantis and its victims; she recalls reading Fabre and repeats the insect’s other names. Christy introduces himself to Curlee and confirms Alexia’s secret marriage. Coralee mistakes Curlee’s sketch of young Kirol for Cletus. Curlee tells Angelic that Alexia is her mother. Both Angelic and Evarista identify their attacker to him, although the names they give are withheld from the reader at this point.

Chapter 14Who never drew the blood of boar

The family reads the poem about La Sorcière. Christy recognizes Kirol’s early handwriting and recalls that Alexia bore the nickname. Alexia acknowledges Kirol’s youthful resemblance to Cletus. A new warning from Kirol precedes another vigil. Alexia proposes taking Clementine upstairs alone, but Curlee insists that Alice accompany them.

Chapter 15The Hawk has caught the fearful Grouse.

Garrity confesses that he attempted to kill Kirol on Alexia’s orders nineteen years earlier. Curlee meets Kirol at a roadhouse and coordinates the final confrontation. At the house, Matt has learned of his sister’s marriage. Alexia knocks Alice down and locks herself in the upstairs parlor with Clementine. Curlee unlocks the door: Kirol is already inside, having freed Clementine from Alexia’s grip. He holds Alexia’s wrists until Alice takes charge. The police release Kirol and detain Alexia.

Chapter 16A bitter ending and condign,

Kirol reveals that he is Matt and Alexia’s third cousin. He recounts Alexia’s illness, the honeymoon attack, Angelic’s birth, the Messina asylum, and the repeated attacks on his daughter. He explains Garrity’s attempt on his life and Christy’s blackmail. He acknowledges that his efforts to shield Alexia were mistaken. He prompted Angelic’s warning and identifies Alexia as Cletus’s poisoner, describing lethal pellets mixed with sleeping pills. Curlee draws a “goat song” while Kirol offers several possible explanations for entering the guarded house. The novel closes on his line: “With a trickster like me, old Carl, old soft-nose, you can never be sure.”

Glossary

Title and Key Terms

MantisGreek

Curlee looks up the word in the dictionary Ch. 10. Evarista tells him that the insect’s victims are never alarmed as they are killed; Curlee later recalls the remark Chs. 13, 15. Evarista cites Fabre, recalling the names “Devil’s Riding Horse,” “Mule Killer,” “Soothsayer,” and “La Sorcière” Ch. 13.

Greek μάντις (mántis): seer, prophet, diviner (LSJ).

La SorcièreFrench

Alexia’s nickname in her New York circle and an allusion in Kirol’s old poem. Kirol calls her “the enchantress, La Sorcière of our group” Chs. 14, 16.

French: the witch or sorceress.

The Monster

Angelic’s name for the woman who attacked her in childhood Ch. 7. The attacker is Alexia Chs. 13, 16.

Lost Dauphin

Ginny’s nickname for Linus, reported by Coralee: he is “the heir apparent to the McKay throne” and “he sure is lost” Ch. 2.

Dragon’s Child

Martha Anne’s affectionate name for Evarista, following her question, “Do I have a dragon’s child?” Ch. 4.

Duchess Joshua

Alexia’s name for Angelic, whom she imagines bringing down Curlee’s defenses as Joshua brought down the walls of Jericho Ch. 3.

Toy Vulture

Kirol’s term for Thomas Christy, Alexia’s blackmailer. He also calls him a “junior vulture” Ch. 16.

Great Mogo

A magician’s name used by Curlee to test Angelic. Curlee claims he has just invented it; Angelic insists Mogo existed and that Curlee has taken the name from her mind Ch. 3.

Seed of Satan / Seed of the Devil

Alexia’s descriptions of Angelic; she later applies “Seed of the Devil” to the McKay family Ch. 16.

Goat SongGreek

Curlee describes his drawing as “a goat song, a very sharp and powerful tragedy” Ch. 16.

Greek τραγῳδία (tragōidia), conventionally rendered “goat song,” is the source of tragedy. See “Tragoedia” in Smith’s Dictionary of Greek and Roman Antiquities.

Romany Vocabulary

PatteranRomany

A trail sign, discussed by Curlee and Alexia Ch. 9. Curlee says it means both a trail and a leaf. Little Willy Ringo pretends to follow these signs to the Lazzaris but already knows where they are Ch. 10.

Feminine of Evaristus, ultimately from Greek εὐάρεστος (euarestos), well-pleasing (Evaristus).

Basil derives from Greek Βασίλειος (Basileios), royal or kingly, from βασιλεύς (basileus), king (Basil).
